Delete your WatchMint account

In the app

Open Settings → Delete account. Sign in again if requested, read the confirmation, and confirm deletion.

Without the app

Email idev.query@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject “WatchMint account deletion”. We may ask you to verify ownership. We aim to complete verified email requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your WatchMint profile, wallet, coin balance, reward ledger, and withdrawal records are deleted. Unused coins are lost. A minimal marker prevents late callbacks from recreating the wallet. Another app account using the shared Firebase sign-in is preserved; shared credentials are deleted when neither app account remains. Advertising providers may retain their own records under their policies.
