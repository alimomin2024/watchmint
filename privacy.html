Privacy policy

This policy applies to WatchMint, operated by Idev (support: idev.query@gmail.com).

Information used

We use your email address, optional profile name, Firebase account ID, reward history, coin balance, and withdrawal details to run your account and review payouts. Authentication credentials are managed by Firebase Authentication.

Our advertising or offer SDKs may process device information, Android advertising ID, IP address, app activity, clicks, and conversion events for ad delivery, attribution, fraud prevention and reward verification. Unity Ads and Yandex Mobile Ads may collect this information under their own policies. TaskMint's offer SDK also uses a device fingerprint and local session records for attribution.

Services and sharing

Google Firebase hosts authentication and app data. Unity Ads and Yandex Mobile Ads supply optional rewarded activities. We send your stable app user ID so completed activities can be credited to the correct account. Withdrawal details are shared with the payout service selected when processing your request. We do not sell your account information.

Choices

Rewarded activities are optional. You can manage your Android advertising ID in system settings, contact support about your data, and request account deletion in Settings or through our deletion page.

Retention and deletion

Account information is retained while your account is active. In-app deletion removes this app's profile, wallet, reward history and withdrawal records. A minimal deletion marker is retained to stop delayed provider callbacks from recreating a deleted wallet. If you use another Idev app sharing the same Firebase sign-in, its account stays available. Shared sign-in credentials are deleted when no account remains in these two apps. Provider-held records are governed by the providers' retention policies.

Security and audience

We restrict wallet writes to server code and protect authenticated requests with Firebase App Check. No system guarantees absolute security. This rewards service is intended for adults aged 18 or older.
